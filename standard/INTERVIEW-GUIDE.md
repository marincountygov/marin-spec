# Marin Spec interview guide

Use these questions in a project discovery conversation. Record the conversation, then give the transcript to your AI tool.

Ask in plain words. Record people by role, such as "project owner" or "front-desk staff", not by name. You do not need to ask every question. Skip what the project owner has already answered. If an answer is "I don't know," write it down. That is a useful answer.

## Project

- What are we trying to build?
- What problem are we trying to solve?
- Why does this matter now?
- Who owns the project?
- Who will look after it once it is live?
- Who should test it before it goes live?

## Users

- Who will use this?
- What are they trying to do?
- What problems do they face today?
- Is this for County staff, the public, or both?

## Current experience

- How does this work today?
- What works well?
- What causes problems?
- What tools or systems are involved?

## Future experience

- What should someone be able to do?
- What should be easier?
- What should happen from start to finish?

## Content and data

- What information does the project need?
- Where does the information come from?
- Who keeps it up to date?
- Is any of it private or sensitive?
- Will the project handle personal information about residents or staff?

## Technology

- What systems must this connect to?
- Are there existing websites, applications, APIs, or repositories involved?

## Access

- Who can see the information?
- Who can create or change information?
- Are there different roles?

## Constraints

- Are there policies, laws, deadlines, technology limits, or other requirements we need to know about?

## Success

- What would make this project successful?
- How will we know it works?

## Before you finish

- Is there anything we did not ask that we should have?
- Who else should we talk to?
- Which documents or examples can you share?
